01 — About

Engineer by discipline,
builder by instinct

I'm a third-year Biomedical and Electrical Engineering student at Carleton University in Ottawa, expected to graduate April 2028. My work spans analog circuit design, FPGA development, and embedded systems.

I'm a Volunteer Research Assistant at SHaRe Lab under Dr. Arash Ahmadi, contributing to the Spiking Neural Network (SNN) team. I'm developing SNN architectures in Python using snnTorch and PyTorch, working with leaky integrate-and-fire neuron models for neuromorphic computing systems targeting low-power adaptive applications.

As Electrical Lead on BIOCARE, Carleton's myoelectric prosthetics team, I lead analog front-end circuit design to condition raw EMG signals for prosthetic gesture control, coordinating electrical decisions across mechanical and software subteams.

I'm actively seeking engineering internships for Fall 2026 in embedded systems, hardware design, and signal processing. I hold a TCPS 2 CORE 2022 Research Ethics certification.
